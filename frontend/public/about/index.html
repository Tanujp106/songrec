About Songrec

Mood-based music recommendations, without the endless scroll.

Songrec helps you discover a track from a handpicked Spotify collection. Pick the mood you are in, adjust how popular you want the pick to be, and get a recommendation that fits the moment.

Try Songrec

What is Songrec?

Songrec is a web app for quick music discovery. It turns a mood choice and a popularity preference into a single track recommendation.

How does Songrec recommend music?

  1. Choose a mood that matches your current vibe.
  2. Set the popularity slider from underrated to popular.
  3. Songrec selects a matching track from its handpicked Spotify collection.

Where do recommendations come from?

Recommendations come from a handpicked Spotify collection that is organized for Songrec's mood and popularity choices. A recommendation links out to Spotify when a track URL is available.
